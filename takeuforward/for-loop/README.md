<p><strong>Input: </strong>low = 1, high = 5</p><p><strong>Output:</strong> 15</p><p><strong>Explanation:</strong> 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 = 15</p>

**Example 2**

<p><strong>Input:</strong> low = 3, high = 7</p><p><strong>Output: </strong>25</p><p><strong>Explanation: </strong>3 + 4 + 5 + 6 + 7 = 25</p>

**Example 3**

<p><strong>Input:</strong> low = 3, high = 8</p>

**Constraints**

<ul><li>0 &lt;= low &lt;= high &lt;= 100</li></ul>
