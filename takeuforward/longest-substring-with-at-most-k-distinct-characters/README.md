# [Longest Substring With At Most K Distinct Characters](https://takeuforward.org/practice/dsa/longest-substring-with-at-most-k-distinct-characters)

<p>Given a string s and an integer k.Find the length of the <strong>longest substring</strong> with at most <strong>k</strong> distinct characters.</p>

**Example 1**

<p><strong>Input :</strong> s = "aababbcaacc" , k = 2</p><p><strong>Output :</strong> 6</p><p><strong>Explanation :</strong> The longest substring with at most two distinct characters is "aababb".</p><p>The length of the string 6.</p>

**Example 2**

<p><strong>Input :</strong> s = "abcddefg" , k = 3</p><p><strong>Output :</strong> 4</p><p><strong>Explanation :</strong> The longest substring with at most three distinct characters is "bcdd".</p><p>The length of the string 4.</p>

**Example 3**

<p><strong>Input :</strong> s = "abccab" , k = 4</p>

**Constraints**

<ul><li>1 &lt;= s.length &lt;= 10<sup>5</sup></li><li>1 &lt;= k &lt;= 26</li></ul>
